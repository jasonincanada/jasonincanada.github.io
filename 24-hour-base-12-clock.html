Base-12 · 24-Hour Clock

The dozenal “second” is 5⁄6 of a standard second
(72 per minute)

X = dek (ten) E = el (eleven) 10 = twelve
Noon is at the top; the hour hand completes one revolution per day.
The subtle inner ring is the 60-minute scale, numbered in dozenal.
Set the standard-time workday with #workday=08:00-16:30 in the URL.
