FieldMuted red marks the next collision asteroid. Follow its animated dashed red route; matching numbers connect opposite edges. The timer assumes you stay at your current position.

PreviewHover or tap the distance graph to see future positions. Back to live returns to the present. Each asteroid leaves a dot every 15 minutes of flight time; up to six dots stay at their recorded positions and fade out over 90 minutes. Preview shows that history at the selected time. Your actual flight continues at normal speed.

AvoidanceCyan marks a checked escape. Take evasive action flies there and stops. The close-up keeps the map's orientation; icons are enlarged for visibility. Close passes follows the graph's selected time, showing nearby asteroids, edge clearance, and closing or receding motion at that instant. PREVIEW marks projected positions; Back to live restores current positions. Its circles use the actual collision radii. The outer ring stays at 3 km, with faint rings at 2 and 1 km. Dashed courses extend across the rectangular view with arrows showing direction.

DistanceThe header labels the distance to contact shown on the graph and the remaining route distance including wraps. Route distance divided by speed gives hours to contact, matching the slide rule and field preview. The graph shows the shortest wrapped distance from the asteroid to its predicted position at first contact, assuming you hold position. Both distances end at that same position and agree on the final approach. The original curve and scales stay fixed from Start to contact; the lime Now marker shows progress, even after reopening. Moving or changing threats starts a new graph. Hover or tap to see time and range and preview future points; past points keep the field live. Numbered lines match the field's wraps. The distance stays continuous across an edge, but its slope can change abruptly. Contact is zero remaining travel; collision prediction still accounts for both radii.

Slide ruleThe C scale wraps across the fixed D scale. Speed on C sits over the remaining travel distance on D, including every wrap up to contact. Read time on D beneath the highlighted C index; the callout shows hours with the decimal placed correctly. Hover or tap the graph to show the route and time remaining from the selected future position. Back to live restores the present; the impact countdown always stays live.

ControlsHold WASD, arrows, or the on-screen keys to move; release to stop. The ship turns smoothly toward its last movement and keeps that heading when stopped. Manual input cancels an assisted move. Flight continues while Help is open.

Background watchFlight auto-saves and catches up while away. Quiet reminders sound at each whole countdown hour, then each minute from one hour remaining. During the final minute, just the higher boop sounds each second, stopping at collision. Use the speaker beside Help to enable or mute reminders; your choice is saved. Waves indicate active audio; a crossed speaker indicates muted or blocked audio. A click or keypress unlocks audio unless you have muted it. Sound needs an awake browser and computer.
